# astrolabe-crib-1 (locked before the dates are pulled)
Logan, live: "crib that" (the astrolabe / Three Stars Each tradition as a crib).
Replace the modern visibility model with the Babylonians' own heliacal rising dates: MUL.APIN Tablet I,
dates in the schematic 360-day year (month m, day d -> day (m-1)*30 + d; Nisannu = month 1).
Cribs (Weidner placements, King slices): Anunitum -> 2; APIN -> 2; SIPA.ZI.AN.NA (Orion) -> 4; MAS.TAB.BA (Twins) -> 5; UGA (Raven) -> 6.
Each crib uses MUL.APIN's listed rising date for that constellation, from a source quoted in the result.
A crib with no MUL.APIN rising date is dropped and reported (not replaced by a modern date).
Wheel: 8 equal slices of 45 schematic days. Free: start day (1-day grid) and direction. Miss in degrees of the wheel.
Null: 2,000 random slice assignments through the same search, seed 20261101. p = share fitting as well.
Pass/fail is not claimed in advance; the result is reported against the ephemeris result (0.6%).

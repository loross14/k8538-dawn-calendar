Logan, live: "now do the final crib with my new thesis and see if we get better than 2% randomness match"

New thesis, made mechanical: the eight slices are equal eighths of the year in TIME (45-day administrative
eighths; 365.2422/8 days in the real year), placed by the date of each constellation's heliacal rising at Nineveh.
Free settings: start day (1-day grid) and direction (+/-1). Nothing else.
Old wheel, for comparison: equal 45-degree bins of the Sun's longitude (heliacal_drag geometry, 2-degree grid).

Cribs (Weidner 1915 placements, King slices), all five:
  Anunitum = Mirach -> 2; APIN as Cassiopeia (Schedar, Caph, Ruchbah) -> 2; Orion (Betelgeuse, Rigel, Bellatrix, Alnilam) -> 4;
  Twins before Orion (Alhena, Alzirr) -> 5; the bird, "perhaps the Raven" = Corvus (Gienah, Kraz, Algorab, Minkar) -> 6.
A crib counts as inside when any member's rising falls in its slice (as in heliacal_drag). Members with no rising are dropped and reported.
Epoch 700 BCE. Miss in degrees of the wheel.
Null: 2,000 random slice assignments (1..8 per crib), each run through the identical best-fit search. Seed 20261031.
p = share of random assignments that fit as well (total miss <= observed).
